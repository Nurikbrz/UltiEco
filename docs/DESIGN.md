# Design language · v0.1

**Figma:** [UltiEco — Brand & Cross-Device UI](https://www.figma.com/design/iYpR16MRFiOzVNPeJsaJsS)

## Identity

- Name: **UltiEco**
- Tagline: **Any device. Any brand. Your rules.**
- Emblem: three connected nodes forming a U to represent independent devices participating in one ecosystem.
- Typeface: **DM Sans** in the concept screens.

## Accessibility & states

Do not rely on color alone for connection status. Design offline, denied, loading, pairing and unsupported states. Verify contrast ratios before implementation. Keep touch targets and keyboard focus visible.

**Everything pictured is conceptual**, not a working app screenshot.
